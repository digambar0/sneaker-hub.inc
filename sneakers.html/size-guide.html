📏 How to Measure Your Feet

1

Prepare

Place a piece of paper on a flat surface against a wall

2

Position

Stand on the paper with your heel against the wall

3

Mark

Mark the longest part of your foot on the paper

4

Measure

Measure the distance from the wall to the mark in CM

💡 Fitting Tips

👣

Measure Both Feet

Your feet may be slightly different sizes. Use the larger measurement.

🕐

Measure in the Evening

Feet tend to swell during the day, so measure when they're largest.

🧦

Wear Socks

Measure with the type of socks you'll wear with the shoes.

📐

Check Width

If you have wide or narrow feet, consider ordering a half size up or down.
