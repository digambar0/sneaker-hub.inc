Returns & Refunds

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase

🔄 Return Policy

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unworn items in original condition with tags attached.

📅

30 Days

Return window from delivery date

💯

Full Refund

Get your money back in full

🆓

Free Returns

On all US orders over $50

📋 How to Return

1

Initiate Return

Log into your dashboard and select the order you want to return

2

Print Label

Download and print your prepaid return shipping label

3

Pack Items

Place items in original packaging with tags attached

4

Ship Back

Drop off at any carrier location or schedule a pickup

5

Get Refund

Receive refund within 5-7 business days after we receive your return

✅ Return Conditions

Items Must Be:

  • Unworn and in original condition
  • In original packaging with all tags attached
  • Returned within 30 days of delivery
  • Free from odors, stains, or damage
  • Accompanied by original receipt or order confirmation

Non-Returnable Items:

  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Worn or damaged items
  • Items without original tags
  • Custom or personalized items

💰 Refund Information

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll process your refund:

Day 1-2

Return received and inspected

Day 3-4

Refund processed and approved

Day 5-7

Refund appears in your account

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

🔁 Exchanges

Need a different size or color? We make exchanges easy!

  • Exchanges are processed as a return + new order
  • Return your original item following the return process
  • Place a new order for the item you want
  • We'll refund your original purchase once we receive the return
  • For faster service, place your new order before returning the original

🌍 International Returns

International customers can return items within 30 days:

  • Customer is responsible for return shipping costs
  • Original shipping fees are non-refundable
  • Customs fees and duties are non-refundable
  • Contact our support team for return instructions

⚠️ Damaged or Defective Items

Received a damaged or defective item? We'll make it right!

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Provide photos of the damage or defect
  • We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund
  • Return shipping is free for damaged/defective items
