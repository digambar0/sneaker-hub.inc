⏱️ Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your order has been shipped with tracking information.

  • Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship the same day
  • Orders placed after 2 PM EST ship the next business day
  • Weekend orders are processed on Monday
  • Holiday processing times may vary

📦 Order Tracking

Track your order easily with our tracking system:

  1. Check your email for the shipping confirmation
  2. Click the tracking link or visit our Track Order page
  3. Enter your order ID and email address
  4. View real-time updates on your delivery status
