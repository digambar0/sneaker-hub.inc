Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about SneakerHub

🛒 Ordering

Browse our collection, select your desired items, choose your size, and click "Add to Cart". Once you're ready, go to your cart and click "Proceed to Checkout". Fill in your shipping and payment information, then confirm your order.

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, orders are processed and cannot be changed. Contact our support team immediately if you need to make changes.

No, you can checkout as a guest. However, creating an account allows you to track orders, save addresses, view order history, and enjoy faster checkout in the future.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All payments are processed securely through encrypted connections.

📦 Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days, Express shipping takes 2-3 business days, and Next Day delivery arrives in 1 business day. International shipping varies by location (10-18 days).

Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all US orders over $100. International orders have different thresholds based on location.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order on our Track Order page using your order ID and email address.

Yes, we ship to over 100 countries worldwide. International shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. See our Shipping Information page for details.

🔄 Returns & Exchanges

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unworn items in original condition with tags attached. Returns are free for US orders over $50. See our Returns page for full details.

Log into your dashboard, select the order you want to return, print the prepaid return label, pack the items with tags attached, and ship them back. You'll receive a refund within 5-7 business days after we receive your return.

Yes! Exchanges are processed as a return + new order. Return your original item and place a new order for the size you want. We'll refund your original purchase once we receive the return.

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We'll send a replacement or issue a full refund immediately. Return shipping is free for damaged or defective items.
